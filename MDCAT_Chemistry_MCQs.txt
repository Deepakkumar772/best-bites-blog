TOPIC 1: ATOMIC STRUCTURE (50 MCQs)
===================================

1. The number of electrons in the outermost shell of carbon is:
A) 2
B) 4
C) 6
D) 8
Answer: B) 4
Explanation: Carbon has atomic number 6, with electronic configuration 2,4, so it has 4 electrons in its outermost shell.

2. Which quantum number describes the shape of an orbital?
A) Principal quantum number (n)
B) Azimuthal quantum number (l)
C) Magnetic quantum number (m)
D) Spin quantum number (s)
Answer: B) Azimuthal quantum number (l)
Explanation: The azimuthal quantum number (l) determines the shape of the orbital (s, p, d, f).

3. The maximum number of electrons in d orbital is:
A) 2
B) 6
C) 10
D) 14
Answer: C) 10
Explanation: The d subshell has 5 orbitals, each can hold 2 electrons, so maximum is 5 × 2 = 10 electrons.

4. Which element has the electronic configuration [Ar] 4s² 3d¹⁰ 4p⁵?
A) Chlorine
B) Bromine
C) Iodine
D) Fluorine
Answer: B) Bromine
Explanation: This configuration corresponds to atomic number 35, which is bromine.

5. The first ionization energy generally increases across a period because:
A) Nuclear charge decreases
B) Atomic radius increases
C) Nuclear charge increases while shielding remains nearly constant
D) Number of shells increases
Answer: C) Nuclear charge increases while shielding remains nearly constant
Explanation: Increased nuclear charge pulls electrons more strongly, requiring more energy to remove them.

TOPIC 2: CHEMICAL BONDING (50 MCQs)
===================================

6. The type of bond present in NaCl is:
A) Covalent
B) Ionic
C) Metallic
D) Hydrogen
Answer: B) Ionic
Explanation: NaCl is formed by electron transfer from Na to Cl, creating ionic bond between Na⁺ and Cl⁻.

7. The shape of NH₃ molecule is:
A) Linear
B) Tetrahedral
C) Pyramidal
D) Bent
Answer: C) Pyramidal
Explanation: NH₃ has 3 bonding pairs and 1 lone pair, giving it a pyramidal shape according to VSEPR theory.

8. Which molecule shows hydrogen bonding?
A) CH₄
B) NH₃
C) SiH₄
D) PH₃
Answer: B) NH₃
Explanation: NH₃ can form hydrogen bonds because nitrogen is highly electronegative and bonded to hydrogen.

9. The bond order in O₂ molecule is:
A) 1
B) 1.5
C) 2
D) 3
Answer: C) 2
Explanation: O₂ has 6 bonding electrons and 2 antibonding electrons, so bond order = (6-2)/2 = 2.

10. Polar covalent bonds are formed between:
A) Two identical atoms
B) Two atoms with similar electronegativity
C) Two atoms with different electronegativity
D) Metal and non-metal only
Answer: C) Two atoms with different electronegativity
Explanation: Electronegativity difference creates partial charges, making the bond polar covalent.

11. At constant temperature, pressure and volume of a gas are:
A) Directly proportional
B) Inversely proportional
C) Independent
D) Equal
Answer: B) Inversely proportional
Explanation: According to Boyle's Law, P ∝ 1/V at constant temperature.

12. The kinetic energy of gas molecules is directly proportional to:
A) Pressure
B) Volume
C) Absolute temperature
D) Density
Answer: C) Absolute temperature
Explanation: Average kinetic energy of gas molecules = (3/2)kT, directly proportional to temperature.

14. The pH of 0.1 M HCl solution is:
A) 0.1
B) 1
C) 10
D) 13
Answer: B) 1
Explanation: pH = -log[H⁺] = -log(0.1) = -log(10⁻¹) = 1.

15. A buffer solution contains:
A) Strong acid and strong base
B) Weak acid and its salt
C) Strong acid and weak base
D) Two strong acids
Answer: B) Weak acid and its salt
Explanation: Buffer solutions resist pH changes and are made from weak acid/base with their salts.

18. The general formula for alkenes is:
A) CₙH₂ₙ₊₂
B) CₙH₂ₙ
C) CₙH₂ₙ₋₂
D) CₙH₂ₙ₋₆
Answer: B) CₙH₂ₙ
Explanation: Alkenes are unsaturated hydrocarbons with one double bond, following CₙH₂ₙ formula.

19. Benzene shows which type of isomerism?
A) Chain isomerism
B) Position isomerism
C) Resonance
D) Optical isomerism
Answer: C) Resonance
Explanation: Benzene has delocalized π electrons that show resonance stabilization.

20. The product of hydration of ethene is:
A) Ethane
B) Ethanol
C) Ethanoic acid
D) Ethyl acetate
Answer: B) Ethanol
Explanation: Hydration adds H₂O across the double bond: CH₂=CH₂ + H₂O → CH₃CH₂OH.
