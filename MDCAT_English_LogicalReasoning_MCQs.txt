TOPIC 1: GRAMMAR (80 MCQs)
===========================

1. Choose the correct form: "She _____ to school every day."
A) go
B) goes
C) going
D) gone
Answer: B) goes
Explanation: Third person singular present tense requires 's' with the verb.

2. Which sentence is in passive voice?
A) John wrote the letter.
B) The letter was written by John.
C) John is writing the letter.
D) John will write the letter.
Answer: B) The letter was written by John.
Explanation: Passive voice follows the structure: object + be + past participle + by + subject.

3. Identify the type of noun in "happiness":
A) Concrete noun
B) Abstract noun
C) Proper noun
D) Collective noun
Answer: B) Abstract noun
Explanation: Happiness is a feeling/quality that cannot be touched, making it abstract.

4. Choose the correct article: "_____ honest man is respected."
A) A
B) An
C) The
D) No article
Answer: B) An
Explanation: "An" is used before words starting with vowel sounds; "honest" starts with silent 'h'.

5. Fill in the blank: "If I _____ rich, I would help the poor."
A) am
B) was
C) were
D) had been
Answer: C) were
Explanation: Second conditional uses "were" for all persons in the if-clause.

TOPIC 1: PATTERN RECOGNITION (40 MCQs)
=======================================

1. Complete the series: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?
A) 40
B) 42
C) 44
D) 46
Answer: B) 42
Explanation: Differences are 4, 6, 8, 10, so next difference is 12. 30 + 12 = 42.

2. Find the odd one out: 8, 27, 64, 125, 144
A) 8
B) 27
C) 64
D) 144
Answer: D) 144
Explanation: All others are perfect cubes (2³, 3³, 4³, 5³), but 144 is 12².

3. Complete: A, C, F, J, O, ?
A) S
B) T
C) U
D) V
Answer: C) U
Explanation: Differences are +1, +3, +4, +5, so next is +6. O + 6 = U.

4. All doctors are intelligent. John is a doctor. Therefore:
A) John is not intelligent
B) John is intelligent
C) John may be intelligent
D) Cannot be determined
Answer: B) John is intelligent
Explanation: If all doctors are intelligent and John is a doctor, then John must be intelligent.

5. If A > B and B > C, then:
A) A < C
B) A = C
C) A > C
D) Cannot be determined
Answer: C) A > C
Explanation: Transitive property: if A > B and B > C, then A > C.
