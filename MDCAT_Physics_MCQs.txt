TOPIC 1: MECHANICS - MOTION & FORCE (60 MCQs)
=============================================

1. A body moving with constant velocity has:
A) Zero acceleration
B) Constant acceleration
C) Variable acceleration
D) Infinite acceleration
Answer: A) Zero acceleration
Explanation: Constant velocity means no change in velocity, so acceleration = Δv/Δt = 0.

2. The SI unit of force is:
A) Dyne
B) Newton
C) Pound
D) Kilogram
Answer: B) Newton
Explanation: Newton (N) is the SI unit of force, defined as kg⋅m⋅s⁻².

3. Newton's first law is also known as:
A) Law of action-reaction
B) Law of inertia
C) Law of momentum
D) Law of gravitation
Answer: B) Law of inertia
Explanation: Newton's first law states that objects resist changes in motion, which is the concept of inertia.

4. If the net force acting on a body is zero, the body will:
A) Stop immediately
B) Move with increasing velocity
C) Continue in its state of rest or uniform motion
D) Move with decreasing velocity
Answer: C) Continue in its state of rest or uniform motion
Explanation: This is a direct statement of Newton's first law of motion.

5. The acceleration due to gravity on Earth is approximately:
A) 9.8 m/s
B) 9.8 m/s²
C) 98 m/s²
D) 0.98 m/s²
Answer: B) 9.8 m/s²
Explanation: Standard acceleration due to gravity is 9.8 meters per second squared.

6. Projectile motion is a combination of:
A) Two uniform motions
B) Two uniformly accelerated motions
C) One uniform and one uniformly accelerated motion
D) Two circular motions
Answer: C) One uniform and one uniformly accelerated motion
Explanation: Horizontal motion is uniform, vertical motion is uniformly accelerated due to gravity.

7. Sound waves are:
A) Transverse waves
B) Longitudinal waves
C) Electromagnetic waves
D) Standing waves
Answer: B) Longitudinal waves
Explanation: Sound waves consist of compression and rarefaction, making them longitudinal.

8. The speed of sound in air at 20°C is approximately:
A) 330 m/s
B) 343 m/s
C) 300 m/s
D) 3×10⁸ m/s
Answer: B) 343 m/s
Explanation: Speed of sound in air at 20°C is about 343 m/s.

9. The phenomenon of bending of waves around obstacles is called:
A) Reflection
B) Refraction
C) Diffraction
D) Interference
Answer: C) Diffraction
Explanation: Diffraction is the bending of waves around obstacles or through openings.

10. Two waves are said to be in phase when their:
A) Frequencies are equal
B) Amplitudes are equal
C) Phase difference is zero
D) Wavelengths are equal
Answer: C) Phase difference is zero
Explanation: Waves are in phase when they reach corresponding points simultaneously.

TOPIC 3: ELECTRICITY & MAGNETISM (50 MCQs)
==========================================

11. The SI unit of electric charge is:
A) Ampere
B) Volt
C) Coulomb
D) Ohm
Answer: C) Coulomb
Explanation: Coulomb (C) is the SI unit of electric charge.

12. Ohm's law states that V = IR, where V is:
A) Current
B) Voltage
C) Resistance
D) Power
Answer: B) Voltage
Explanation: In Ohm's law, V represents voltage or potential difference.

13. Two resistors of 4Ω and 6Ω are connected in parallel. Their equivalent resistance is:
A) 10Ω
B) 2.4Ω
C) 5Ω
D) 1.5Ω
Answer: B) 2.4Ω
Explanation: 1/R = 1/4 + 1/6 = 5/12, so R = 12/5 = 2.4Ω.

14. The magnetic field around a current-carrying conductor is:
A) Radial
B) Circular
C) Linear
D) Parabolic
Answer: B) Circular
Explanation: Magnetic field lines form concentric circles around a current-carrying conductor.
